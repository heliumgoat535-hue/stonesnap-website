Luna Marchetti

Creator of Stone Snap · Rock Collector · Geology Enthusiast

Luna has been collecting rocks since she could walk. Shoe boxes full of quartz. Pockets full of gravel her parents didn't appreciate. A college geology textbook that fell apart from overuse.

After years of hauling field guides on hikes and getting vague results from general-purpose image search tools, she decided to build something better. Stone Snap was born from that frustration: a dedicated AI rock identifier that actually understands geology.

Background

Luna studied geology and environmental science, spending years in the field identifying specimens the old-fashioned way: hand lens, streak plate, hardness tests, and heavy reference books. She collected over 800 specimens from trails, riverbeds, and road cuts across the country.

When large language models with vision capabilities became available through Google's AI, she saw the opportunity to build the tool she always wanted: point your phone at a rock and get a real geological breakdown. Not a Wikipedia link. Not "this might be a mineral." Actual data — chemical composition, Mohs hardness, crystal system, formation environment, and industrial uses.

She built Stone Snap as an Android app, added Rocky the AI chat assistant for follow-up questions, created a learning hub with quizzes and flashcards, and included a kid mode so young explorers could use it too. The app launched on Google Play in 2026.
